# Status and Roadmap

## Current status

**Early-stage topic selection.**

This repository supports an open-data health-services research scaffold focused on county-level typologies of cancer place of death and end-of-life care intensity. It remains a topic-selection and variable-mapping workspace until the dataset, outcome definition, and linkage strategy are frozen.

## Public-facing status language

Use the following wording in CV, portfolio, and public research-status outputs:

> Cancer end-of-life typologies scaffold aligned to place-of-death, cancer care, and end-of-life quality variation; dataset, typology outcome, variable map, and causal boundaries remain pending.

## Near-term roadmap

1. Select the defensible public dataset or dataset combination.
2. Define the typology outcome and geographic unit.
3. Map candidate variables for hospice access, care intensity, rurality, socioeconomic context, and healthcare supply.
4. Create a claims-boundary note that avoids unsupported causal framing.
5. Build a source and variable crosswalk.
6. Draft a short methods concept note before expanding manuscript prose.

## Validation gates

- Do not use causal language before design and identification limits are documented.
- Keep county-level ecological limits explicit.
- Preserve all source, vintage, and linkage assumptions.

## Roadmap priority

Medium-low priority until the main submitted manuscript, McDowell work, and biomedical benchmark scaffolds are stabilized.
