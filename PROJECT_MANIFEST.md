# Project Manifest

## Repository role

This repository is an open-data health-services research scaffold for county-level typologies of cancer place of death and end-of-life care intensity.

## Current status

Early-stage topic selection.

## Public CV wording

Cancer end-of-life typologies scaffold aligned to place-of-death, cancer care, and end-of-life quality variation; dataset, typology outcome, variable map, and causal boundaries remain pending.

## Primary objects

- Public dataset decision record
- Typology outcome definition
- Geographic-unit definition
- Candidate variable crosswalk
- Hospice access and healthcare supply inputs
- Rurality and socioeconomic context inputs
- Claims-boundary note
- Short methods concept note

## Roadmap priority

Priority 7. Promising health-services research scaffold, but lower priority until higher-priority submitted manuscript, infrastructure, and biomedical benchmark tracks are stabilized.

## Validation boundary

Keep county-level ecological limits explicit. Do not use causal language until design, linkage, and inference limits are documented.
